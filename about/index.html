About

Since January 2024, scruffian has written a song every day. That's 1000 songs. Weaving intricate finger-picking patterns with melancholy melodies and reflective lyrics, these songs act as a kind of diary, tracking the ups and downs of the last few years. They are songs of the sun, the rain, the river, the wild; songs of hope, fear, joy, sadness and love.
