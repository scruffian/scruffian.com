thursday, november 13

today when i left the house i unplugged my mobile from the charger and plugged tim's in. i realised that he's had it back from whoever found it as wisley. it set me thinking. before i got into trail riding i used to be scared of dirt jumpers. i thought they would all hate me because i was rubbish and that they are totally full of themselves and thought everyone else was crap. i am glad i got into trails.

when we went to kiddy in the summer i was still a bit reserved because i'd not met that many other trail riders before. it was a really good suprise. since then i have realised what a good group of people dirt riders are. simon built me a computer and some random guy who we've never met still sent tim's phone back. i guess all the people who have built trails that i have ridden have given me a lot as well. i'm not saying that everyone who ever rides trails is automatically a nice person, but i guess because these people dig is shows that they are willing to put in a lot of work to build something as a community.

there's not really a point to this, just some thoughts. i suppose the point should be that this is good and we should help each other out more often. or something cheesy like that. here's a picture of the summer taken at about 9 at night. the ground was wetter then than it is now (went digging last night).
