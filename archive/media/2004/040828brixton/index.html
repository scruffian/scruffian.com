Brixton Bowls:

The bowls are all
very mellow but
there are lots of
lines. I only rode
a few of them,
but there must
have been a lot
more. The locals
really look after
this place well.

Considering it's
practically in
central London
somehow they
have their own
sofas and chairs
and stuff.

There's a lot of
ghetto ramps
built by the
locals as well.

The whole place
has a really good
atmosphere quite
different to what
you expect when
you hear stories
about the park.

It's fun to ride as
well but it took me
a while to get
used to it.

these pictures.

because I can't be bothered and it
