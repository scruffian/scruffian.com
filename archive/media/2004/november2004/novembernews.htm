Today's date is 25th November 2004. Thirty years ago today Nick Drake was found dead in his parents home in Tanworth in Arden, Warwickshire. (pictured below)

Nick Drake was born in Burma on 19 June, 1948 and moved to Britain when he was seven. Through out his life he wrote many beautiful songs, and recorded three albums, the first of which (called Five Leaves Left) was recorded in 1970.
 
He went on to record a second album, in 1972, called Bryter Layter. After a quiet spell in Europe, he returned to Britian and approached his producer, asking to record a new album. The studio was too busy in the day, so Nick went to the studio at 11 after it was shut. He recorded Pink Moon in two nights, accompanied solely by his acoustic guitar. The final track, From The Morning, is engraved on his tombstone.

Due to his shyness, Drake found live performances too difficult and found himself in a spiral of depression. This, combined with the commercial failure of his music, served to seriously blacken his mood. Once again Drake escaped to Europe. His bouts of depression were said to have diminished after he decided to live there permanently. 

In 1974 He returned to his parents house in Britain and began recording a new album. He appeared to be over his depression, but during the early hous of 25 Novemeber 1974, Nick overdosed on Tryptasol, an anti-depressant. Nick would often go downstairs to take some sleeping pills, and some people argue that he may have taken the wrong ones. No-one had told Nick that an overdose of Tryptasol could be fatal. The coroner's verdict was suicide but relatives and acquaintances have always felt that his overdose of prescription drugs was accidental. Whether Nick thought he was taking Tryptasol or sleeping pills, he still must have taken a lot.

There has been a lot written about Nick Drake on the internet, and it makes quite interesting reading, but to me his music always outshines the mystery surrounding his life and death, and this is how I think it should be. If you have never heard Nick's music before then go and listen to it. Nick's Producer said something like this:

"Maybe once or twice, I have played someone Nick's music and they have said, Yeh, it's ok. Maybe once or twice. Every other time they have been amazed by what they heard."

Shark Update

I have not done a shark update for a very long time as I have been very busy being a wife and earning Ben some money.  But due to popular demand here is a shark update.  I hope you enjoy it.  It is about the shark with the most amusing name:  The wobbegong shark.  They don't tend to eat people, which makes them a lot less interesting.

They are quite funny looking though:

And they will bite you if you stand on one.  Which is understandable.  Yesterday I saw a lady who had lost her teeth but was looking forward to getting them back so she could bite people yum yum yum.

Some information about wobbegong sharks (orectolobus ornatus)

Size - 10 feet long

Diet - The wobbegong has wormlike projections around its mouth that it uses to suck prey into razor teeth.  Umm, that might hurt quite a bit.

Habitat - indigenous to Australia and the coastal reefs of the Pacific, on the ocean floor waiting for fish to approach.

Reproduction - the female hatches about 20 pups within her body, then gives birth to them.  Not so different from humans then.
