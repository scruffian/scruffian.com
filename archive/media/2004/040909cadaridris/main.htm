Climbing up a mountain
When I was in Wales I went for a walk up a mountain called Cadar Idirs. Here are some photos: Steps, and a waterfall.

That looks like the top, but when we got there we realised that it wasn't so we had to go somewhere else.

If you look to the left you can see the gully we walked up. That's not the path but we went the wrong way.

This is the lake at the foot of the mountain. Well it was about half way up and it was very cold and very clear.

It was very tiring walking to the top. Becky's team went the wrong way. Where's Wally?

This is the top. Worth it! The view was actually much better up here than you can get on camera. There was a hut thing too.
