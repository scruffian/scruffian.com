the sky is really big. sometimes i like to just lie on the the ground and look at it, usually after i've stacked, which is pretty often. the sky is so huge but you usually just look at the ground or other stuff and never really notice it, except sunsets which people love. sunsets are cool, but the sky is good all the time.
i remember when i was doing my art a-level we went on a trip to a gallery in london. i think it was the tate - the old one. when we had finished we sat on a bench outside and looked at the sky for a bit. we realised that the sky was actually more interesting to look at than the art work inside the building. it's far more detailed and it is constantly changing.

this started a debate over whether the sky was art. i can't remember the outcome, all i know is that i enjoy looking at it a lot more than most artwork
