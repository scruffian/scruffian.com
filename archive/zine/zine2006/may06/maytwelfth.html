Its not easy to grow blue bells in clay soil.

Its been nice weather, pretty much perfect, slight breeze and nice and warm.

I don't have much news but I have been thinking about happiness. Its much easier to be unhappy than happy. There are usually reasons for being unhappy but I think you should try and be happy anyway. If you can change things, then do, if you can't then make the best of it. It's a bit like when you see someone who is much better than you at riding getting annoyed becasue they can't do a trick, which you could never do in a million years. You think they are stupid for getting annoyed since they are way better than you anyway. The whole thing is stupid. You should be riding for fun not riding to get better than someone else or to show off. That's not to say you shouldn't get better, but I think if you are having fun you'll get better quicker anyway. This doesn't just apply to riding it applies to everything, like playing music. Don't do it to show off or be better than someone else. Do it for yourself and for fun. Also don't ever forget how lucky you are to have food in the fridge and a bed to sleep in.
