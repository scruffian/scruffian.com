Scruffian

Some things have been happening.
I have been able to tear myself away from the computer when I am at home, as I spend enough time on it when I am at work.
It's easier when you have an open fire and a guitar.

I have written about 3 songs this month. I have enjoyed it too which is good, in the past it has been hard work.  I am not sure what to do with them though. I am scared to let people listen to them. Especially people on the internet.

I have been very scared of the internet recently and have been thinking about not doing this anymore. I guess I take people's criticism too seriously, but you do begin to wonder what the point is. Actually what is the point?

There have been a few trips to Sittingbourne too. Might update with some riding pictures one day.

Other things I have been doing: Collecting Firewood, digging jumps, watching  sunsets and  more sunsets
