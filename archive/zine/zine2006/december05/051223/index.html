The shortest day has been and gone. No-one seems to have noticed. It was on Wednesday. This is good news as it is already getting lighter and spring is on the way. bad news though if you have been slack at the trails. Digging season is already about half gone. In the winter you all moan that you want the summer and in the summer you want the winter. Just be happy with what it is at the time, and make the most of it. That means using a spade for the next 3 months.
