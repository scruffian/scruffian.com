I am listening to Bob Dylan right now. Winter is quite relaxing. It's good to hibernate, playing the guitar, listening to music and generally doodling. Bob Dylan is pretty amazing. His songs are hard to listen to at first but you have to work at them a bit and they just get better. Me and Bob don't like cars too much these days. I don't want a car anymore. I think private motor cars should be made illegal. That way everyone would have to use public transport and it would be more efficient and cheaper. It would solve a lot of other problems too.

I have brought two new tools too. A long handled spade and a shovel. I will never dig again without these. They are so much better. The long handled spade means you can dig for a long time without getting tired as its a lot easier. The shovel pretty much doubles productivity as it hold so much earth. The stainless steel spade is still my favourite for shaping and packing. It's strong and light, but the others are good too.
