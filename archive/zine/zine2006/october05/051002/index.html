It's that time of year again.
Autumn is pretty. It's nice to not overheat
when you ride. The jumps are hard and
smooth. The only thing that gets
to me is when the jumps are too wet to ride
and too dry to dig.

It's time to start some new jumps for me.
It's dark at seven now which doesn't give
you much time after work. Eating can be
left until nightfall. I think my life is much
more food oriented than most.

I have been playing the guitar a lot recently,
and have made up a few new songs.

The zine seems to be working out ok. It's easy to update, fun to make and forces me to write things, which yak will like.
