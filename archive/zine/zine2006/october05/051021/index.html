Friday 21st October. Went to stay in a hotel. I think it should have cost about £200 but we got it on the cheap. It was funny eating in the restaurant with lots of posh
rich people and us in our scruffy clothes. We got the meal free but we had to buy wine. It was too expensive so we chose the cheapest bottle and took it home with us.
You can see it on the window sill. You can also see a door into the bathroom of the room next door. It wasn't well sound proofed and when the people next door got up
we could hear them. It was two guys sharing a room and we could hearing them on the toilet making straining and plopping noises. They were brushing their teeth
together and one said "I like the way you turn off the water when you brush your teeth", to which the other replied "yes my wife insists on that". Make of that what
you will. Enough already.
