24th April. Back to work today.
When you are at school you tend to get annoyed about having to do the same thing everyday. I guess you look forward to long holidays. Going to work is better than school - you get paid, and you don't get homework, but the lack of holidays is pretty bad. Doing the same thing everyday tends to get quite depressing. You start wondering what the point is. This isn't going anywhere. I shouldn't complain really, my job is good and I am not hungry, I have somewhere to live and a 2 day weekend.

  The stupid thing is that I would happily lower my
  standard of living in order to work less, but this
  doesn't seem to be an option in this consumer world.
