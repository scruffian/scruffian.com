april 10th | home | zine | april | just a picture

when i left the house today it was sunny
then i started noticing blocks of ice on the road
the more i drove the snowier it got.
i was having to dodge fallen trees and stuff
at work there was about 6 inches of snow.

this has made a bit of a flood
so i have been making gates instead of mud lumps
grinding and drilling. i wore a drill piece right down.
i think it probably used the wrong kind

i saw a tv program about kids who choose not to speak
they are called selective mutes.
becky knows the lady that was on it who helps them to talk
i was thinking i could probably be a selective mute
i don't say many words in one day
perhaps i will count them

i say hello and goodbye at work
and sometimes i talk to barry
sometimes i shout at the radio in the car
most of my communication is done on a keyboard.
