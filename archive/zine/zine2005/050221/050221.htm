Despite all this snow around I think it is fair to say that summer is definitely on the horizon. It's still not too late to start digging. Or if you are digging there is still time to get stuff finished off.

No one can force you to go out and pick up a spade, but I think that's what's so great. You can't buy good trails. It takes hours of work even if you use a digger. How good your trails are is really only dependent on how hard you work.

Hate something. Change something. Or don't, it's up to you.
