2nd May

Well that was April. In terms of riding it was probably the best April I have ever had. Maybe April 2003 was better but probably not.

The only thing worse than riding on your own at the trails is riding with a load of chavs heckling you. That was Monday. Then after they left a couple of people came to the trails and watched and "took their hats off" to all the hard work we have done.

Yesterday I was in London with work and we went to The Comedy Store after. It was pretty funny. Then on the 22:30 train home I saw one of the people that took off their hats to me the day before at the trails, which was a coincidence.
