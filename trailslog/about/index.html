This website is a collection of pictures, words and ideas which document the lives of people who don't like killing time in front of the television. I like being outside and looking at trees and animals. I like going for walks and taking pictures of things I see. I also like to build jumps out of mud and ride them on my bike. I also like doodling on paper, on the computer and on my guitar.

The site is split into these sections:
How can a I add a bot test to my guestbook to stop spammers?
You can't. Well not easily anyway. You have to get a new guestbook.

Go to carbonize.co.uk and download the Lazarus guestbook. It has instructions with it. This should eliminate most guestbook spam.

Can you give me directions to ******** trails?
No.

No-one wants directions to their trails on the internet. Trails are delicate and it is unfortunate that there are riders out there who can't be trusted to give them the respect they deserve.

If you really want to ride some trails build some yourself. You will soon discover the amount of work required, and will learn how important it is to respect them.

You guys suck.
That's not a question. If you don't like it, don't look.
