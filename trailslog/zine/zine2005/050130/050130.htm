Its hard to talk about trails. A lot of people say things like "put in a bit of digging and you'll be riding jumps like these". Unfortunately this is not true. You might dig for hours in all weathers and then just as the jumps start to dry out something goes wrong and suddenly there are no jumps. I lost four sets of trails like this last year. This hasn't put me off digging again this year and now the trails are under threat again. How long this will go on for I don't know. It is disheartening to see your work destroyed and it makes you wonder if it is worth it. I can tell you how much fun trails
 

are but to understand you have to go and dig some for yourself.
