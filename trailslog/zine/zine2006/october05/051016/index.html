There are too many idiots in bmx. I don't like to think of myself as a bmxer, it's just something I do, not who I am. A lot of people probably hate me for saying that, as the magazines tell you that bmx should be your life and your lifestyle. I don't buy into that, and I don't want to be associated with the bmxers on the pretty shady book. People are my friends because of what they are like not how good they are on a bike.*
