friday, october 24

Agaetis Byrjun
-Sigur Ros

i have been getting up at 5:30 and leaving by 6 in the morning to get to work early and miss the traffic. the journey is just over an hours, which coincidently is the length of this cd. it is perfectly suited to listen to at that time in the morning, as it is quiet and the sun rises. not only that but it's really good.

i must have listened to it every day for at least a week if not more. i remember when i say them play in oxford. people laughed at him because they thought he was singing out of tune, but i think it sounds amazing. if you like this you'll love radiohead. (not necessarily the other way round i'm afraid.)
